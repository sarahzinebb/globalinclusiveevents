Workshops and online conferences for everyone.

Practical, friendly sessions led by practitioners. In person in Morocco, and live online every month, with captions and recordings for those who cannot attend live.

  • When you sign up, tell us what you need.
  • Many sessions are free.

In person

Workshops

Two to three hours, small groups, hands-on. Venues are checked for step-free access, and every registration includes an access-needs question.

Online

Inclusive Voices: a monthly online conference

One live session each month with practitioners, partners and participants from Morocco, Africa and beyond. Watch from anywhere, on any device.

  • Live captions on every session, and sign language interpretation where possible
  • Recording and transcript shared with every registered participant
  • Questions by chat or by voice, whichever is easier for you
  • Optional certificate of attendance

Accessible Event Planning 101

Monthly Mentor Circle

A small live group with an experienced mentor, focused on women entrepreneurs.
