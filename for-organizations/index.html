For organizations

Make your events, venues and teams inclusive.

Morocco is preparing to host major international events in the years ahead, including the 2030 FIFA World Cup with Spain and Portugal. Conferences, venues, hotels and fan experiences will need to welcome every visitor. We help you get there, with the event know-how of Global Premium Events behind us.

Services

What we offer

Assess

Accessibility audit

We review your venue, registration, signage, content and staff practices against recognised accessibility principles, and give you a prioritised, costed action plan.

Commit

Inclusive Event standard

A clear set of commitments your event can meet and communicate: access-needs registration, accessible layout, captioned content, trained staff and participant feedback.

Train

Staff and volunteer training

Practical sessions for hosts, security, front desk and volunteers on welcoming guests with disabilities, with sign language basics.

Deliver

On-site inclusion services

Captioning, sign language interpretation, accessible signage, reserved seating, quiet spaces and trained guides, planned and run on the day.

How it works

From first call to a better event

  1. Listen

    We learn about your event, your audience and your constraints.

  2. Assess

    We audit the venue and the participant journey, before, during and after.

  3. Equip

    We train your teams and put the right tools and services in place.

  4. Measure

    We collect participant feedback and report on what changed.
