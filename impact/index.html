Impact & origins

From Rabat to Bloomington, and back.

Global Inclusive Events started as a question inside Global Premium Events: who is still missing from our rooms? We took that question to Indiana University’s Kelley School of Business, and the answers became our programs.

Read the Kelley School feature → (opens the Kelley website)

A letter from our founder

From exclusivity to inclusivity

After years of hosting international events that brought together brilliant minds, we discovered that our gatherings were doing far more than facilitating networking: they were changing lives. Yet a glaring gap emerged. Women, young individuals from rural areas, and students with disabilities were consistently missing from the transformative circle.

This realization sparked our journey from exclusivity to inclusivity. We reimagined our events as catalysts for change, determined to create spaces where every community member is empowered to thrive.

Sarah Zineb Boumouja

CEO & Co-Founder, Global Premium Events · Kelley School of Business alumna

The partnership

Eight weeks, five teams, one real client

Through a Client-Based International Project (CLIP) grant from IU Global, Global Premium Events served as the real-world client for BUS-X190: Hot Topics — Building a Better World through Inclusive Design, taught by Benjamin Ale-Ebrahim, PhD.

We brought four case studies to the classroom: inclusivity in event planning, women-focused events beyond symbolism, accessibility for students with disabilities, and bridging the digital divide for older generations.

What the students built

Five teams, five solutions

Each solution now lives inside one of our three program pillars.

01
Digital Inclusion

Digital Divide: From Morocco and Beyond

Curtis Yang, Pranay Sen & Mazzy Gallentine

A phased mentorship program pairing young volunteers with older professionals to build AI and digital literacy.

02
Accessibility

Small Changes, Big Impact

Alan Sun, Ameer Takieddin, Anand Bhogal & Andrew Vendt

Reserved aisle seating, wider pathways, accessible signage and volunteer training, mapped against real attendee personas.

03
Accessibility

Inclusive by Design: Accessibility Request Cards

Tess Trieu & Eleanor Beck

An accommodation-request card in registration that lets attendees privately flag access needs before they arrive.

04
Women Empowerment

Beyond Celebration

Angelina Gillette, Susanna van Eeden & Agnes Kong

A showcase pairing women entrepreneurs with speakers, mentors and investors, with subtitled, multilingual content.

05
Women Empowerment

Empowerment Kits

Aanya Arora, Raina Patel, Sara Halliburton & Siya Chava

Monthly digital kits of workshops, mentorship and career tools around Educate, Empower and Celebrate.

Also presented at the same showcase: “Making Global Premium Events Inclusive for the Hard of Hearing” and “Building an Inclusive Future”.
