Privacy notice

How we handle the information you share with us. Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

Global Inclusive Events is an initiative of Global Premium Events, based in Rabat, Morocco. Contact: info@globalpremiumevents.com, +212 653512995.

What we collect

Only what you send us through our forms or by email: your name, contact details, the program or service you are interested in, your message and, if you choose to share them, your access needs. We use access-needs information only to plan your participation.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and register you for sessions
  • To plan accessibility arrangements you asked for
  • To send you the session dates you requested

Who receives it

Our team only. Form messages are delivered to our mailbox by a form-delivery service (FormSubmit). The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are deleted within 24 months unless you take part in a program, in which case we keep your details for the length of the program and our reporting obligations.

Your rights

Under Moroccan Law No. 09-08, and the GDPR for visitors in the European Union, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to info@globalpremiumevents.com. You may also contact the Moroccan data-protection authority, the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).
