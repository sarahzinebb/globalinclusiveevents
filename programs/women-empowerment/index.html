Program · Case 02

From one-day celebration to year-round opportunity.

Too many women-focused events stop at symbolic gestures, with support concentrated on March 8th. Women, especially in rural areas, need year-round access to information, mentors and pathways to economic empowerment.

Join or sponsor this program →Related workshops

The challenge

Why this program exists

Sponsors often fund a single day. We design programs that sponsors can commit to all year, with measurable outcomes in education, entrepreneurship and leadership.

Key question

How can women-focused “celebratory” events become large-scale, high-impact initiatives that deliver real opportunity?

Who: Women aged 18+, girls Where: Morocco & Africa

What we do

The program

  • Monthly Empowerment Kits: workshops, mentorship and career tools built around Educate, Empower and Celebrate
  • An annual showcase that connects women entrepreneurs with speakers, mentors and investors
  • Multilingual mentor matching and subtitled content for rural reach
  • Mobile-first formats that work on low bandwidth
  • Year-round sponsorship packages instead of one-day visibility

This program grew out of Case 02, “Beyond Celebration: Creating Transformational Events for Women”, studied by Kelley students in 2025.

Where it comes from
