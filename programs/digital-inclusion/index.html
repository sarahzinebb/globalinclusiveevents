Program · Case 04

Two generations, one digital future.

Technology moves fast, and many experienced professionals struggle to keep up despite the wisdom they bring. Young people master the tools. We bring them together so both sides grow.

Join or sponsor this program →Related workshops

The challenge

Why this program exists

Age inclusion is an overlooked pillar of inclusion. Training must adapt to different levels of digital literacy and learning speed, and young mentors gain leadership experience in return.

Key question

How can training programs reintegrate experienced professionals into today’s technological landscape?

Who: Young mentors, experienced professionals Where: Morocco & Africa

What we do

The program

  • A 10-week program of training, mentorship and networking
  • Young volunteers paired one-to-one with experienced professionals
  • AI and digital literacy modules adapted to each learner’s level
  • Certificates for both mentors and learners
  • A final networking event that connects both generations with employers

This program grew out of Case 04, “Bridging the Digital Divide for Older Generations”, studied by Kelley students in 2025.
