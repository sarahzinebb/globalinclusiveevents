Accessibility

Accessibility statement

An organization that works for inclusion should have a website everyone can use. Here is what we have done, and how to tell us when something does not work for you.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this website.

What this website does

  • Text and colour contrast designed to meet WCAG AA
  • An “Accessibility” menu at the top of every page: larger text, a more readable font with extra spacing, high contrast, underlined links, paused motion and a light mode that saves mobile data by hiding photos
  • A “Listen” button that reads the page aloud using your device’s voice
  • The website in three languages: English, French and Arabic
  • A short “In simple words” summary at the top of the main pages
  • Contact by WhatsApp, phone or email, whichever is easiest for you
  • Full keyboard navigation, a “skip to main content” link and visible focus
  • Descriptive text for meaningful images
  • Forms with clear labels, and simple tick boxes to tell us what you need to take part
  • Respect for your device’s “reduce motion” setting

What we are still working on

We plan to add short videos in Moroccan Sign Language for key pages. Recordings of online conferences are shared with captions and transcripts.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is difficult to use, please write to info@globalpremiumevents.com or call +212 653512995. We will reply within five business days and provide the information in another format if you need it.
