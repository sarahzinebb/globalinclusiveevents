Our programs

Three pillars. One standard of inclusion.

Each pillar grew out of a real case study and a real student solution, and each one runs year-round, not just on a single awareness day.

Pillar 01

Accessibility

Events where people with visual, hearing and physical disabilities take part fully, with dignity and without friction.

  • Accessibility request cards at registration
  • Layouts, seating and signage that work
  • Trained volunteers and staff
Explore Accessibility →
Pillar 02

Women Empowerment

Year-round access to skills, mentors and investors for women and girls, especially in rural areas.

  • Monthly Empowerment Kits
  • Showcase events with mentors and investors
  • Subtitled, multilingual content
Explore Women Empowerment →
Pillar 03

Digital Inclusion

Young volunteers and experienced professionals learning digital and AI skills side by side.

  • 10-week intergenerational mentorship
  • AI and digital literacy training
  • Networking across generations
Explore Digital Inclusion →

Learn with us

Workshops and online conferences, open to all.

Practical sessions in person and live online every month. Every online session is captioned and recorded, and every in-person session is planned with accessibility in mind.

  • Free for young people, women and community members, thanks to our sponsors
  • Certificate tracks for professionals
  • Private sessions for organizations and their teams

Impact & origins

Built with students, tested on real events.

For eight weeks, Global Premium Events served as the real-world client for Kelley’s course Hot Topics: Building a Better World through Inclusive Design. Five student teams turned our four case studies into concrete solutions, and those solutions are now our programs.
